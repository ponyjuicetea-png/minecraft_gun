## Poly Haven surfaces

The downloaded source images are CC0-1.0: https://polyhaven.com/license

Asset pages, exact image URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `assets/sources.json`.

The texture generation script downsamples these images and derives tinted concrete, asphalt, metal/container, stripe and spawn-pad variants. The world texture pack replaces only the explicitly mapped VoxeLibre textures, not every game texture. The generation code is original GPL-3.0-or-later code; the adapted CC0 surface images remain CC0.

## Original assets

The weapons' OBJ geometry, inventory icons, crosshair, hit marker, target, and synthesized firearm/reload/empty OGG sounds are generated by `scripts/generate_assets.py`. They are original project assets under GPL-3.0-or-later. No Mojang textures or TaCZ assets were copied into the Frontline mod.
